01

It was never a career choice

José Luis grew up in a Dominican barbershop. His father was a barber, so was his grandfather, and so was his great-grandfather before that. He started cutting as a child, which is why he says the trade is in his blood rather than on his CV. Twenty years later he is still behind the chair, most days, himself.

02

Other people's salons first

After moving to the Czech Republic he worked in a string of salons around Prague, learning how the city cuts and what Czech customers expect, before he opened a room of his own on Nuselská. The shop is what he wanted the other ones to be.

03

The clippers and the needle

Alongside barbering, José tattoos, and he does the permanent makeup as well. Most shops pick one. Putting all three under a single badge is the reason the business exists in the shape it does, and it is why somebody who came in for a fade leaves with a consultation booked.

04

What the name means

Más dos 8

Spanish for more than two, because nobody gets through life on their own. The eight on its side is infinity: a cycle with no end and no limit. The name carried the idea before the sign went up.
