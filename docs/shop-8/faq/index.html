01

Do I need to book?

Book if you can. Three of the reviews on our page are about the old booking system letting people down, a closed door with no answer, a wait with barbers standing idle, a barber swapped without warning. This one holds a real slot in the diary, so none of that happens again.

02

Do you speak English or Spanish?

Our own customers write their reviews in Czech, Spanish and English, so come in whichever you’re most comfortable with. José is Dominican, and more than one review mentions feeling at home speaking Spanish here.

03

I don’t know exactly what I want yet.

That’s what the tattoo consultation is for, and it’s free. Bring a reference, a rough idea, or nothing at all, and we agree the size and the price together before the needle starts.

04

Do you cut children’s hair?

Yes. There’s a separate price for under 5s and for 6 to 12s, and a coffee for the parent while it happens.

05

Are the prices real?

Every price you see is the one we charge, all of them, with nothing added at the till. If a service isn’t listed, ask and we’ll tell you the price before you sit down.

06

I need to move or cancel my slot.

Call or WhatsApp us and we’ll move it for you. The reservation reference we give you at the end makes it a thirty second call.
