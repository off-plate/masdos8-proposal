How the shop got here

Santo Domingo

It was never a career choice

His father was a barber. So was his grandfather, and his great-grandfather before that. José grew up in the shop and started cutting as a child, which is why he says the trade is in his blood rather than on his CV.

Prague

Several other people's salons first

After moving to the Czech Republic he worked in a string of salons, learning how the city cuts and what Czech customers expect, before he opened a room of his own.

Two trades

The clippers and the needle

Alongside barbering, José tattoos. Most shops pick one. Putting both under a single badge is the reason the business is called what it is, and it is why customers who came in for a fade leave with a consultation booked.

Today

The team keeps growing

New barbers are trained in the shop rather than hired ready-made, so the way you are looked after is the same whoever picks up the clippers. Dominican energy, as José puts it, and a drink while you wait.

Más dos 8

Más dos is Spanish for more than two, because nobody gets through life on their own. The eight on its side is infinity: a cycle with no end and no limit. That is the whole brand, and it was there before the sign went up.
