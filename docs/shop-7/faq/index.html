Questions we actually get.

If yours isn't here, ask us on WhatsApp and we will give you a proper answer.

Booking

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Book if you can. Three of the reviews on our page are about the old booking system letting people down, a closed door with no answer, a wait with barbers standing idle, a barber swapped without warning. This reservation is built to fix exactly that: it holds a real slot in the diary.

Languages

Do you speak English or Spanish?

Our own customers write their reviews in Czech, Spanish and English, so come in whichever you're most comfortable with. José is Dominican, and more than one review mentions feeling at home speaking Spanish here.

Tattoo

I don't know exactly what I want yet.

That's what the consultation is for, and it's free. Bring a reference, a rough idea, or nothing at all, and we agree the size and the price together before the needle starts.

Kids

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes. There's a separate price for under 5s and for 6 to 12s, and a coffee for the parent while it happens.

Prices

Are the prices on the site the real ones?

Every price you see is the one we charge, all of them, with nothing added at the till. If a service isn't listed, ask and we'll tell you the price before you sit down.

Getting here

How do I get to Nuselská?

Nuselská 133/134, 140 00 Prague 4, Michle. Tram to Nuselská, or five minutes on foot from Vršovická. Open every day from 09:00, and if it's your first visit, call ahead and José will meet you at the door.

Change of plan

I need to move or cancel my slot.

Call or WhatsApp us and we'll move it for you. The reservation reference we give you at the end makes it a thirty second call.
