How the shop got here

Santo Domingo

It was never a career choice

His father was a barber. So was his grandfather, and his great-grandfather before that. José grew up in the shop and started cutting as a child, and as he puts it, the passion for this trade is in his blood.

Prague

Until he could open his own

After moving to the Czech Republic he worked in salons around the city, learning how Prague cuts and what Czech customers like, and saving for a room of his own. He found it on Nuselská.

Two trades

The clippers and the needle

Alongside barbering, José tattoos, and he does the permanent makeup as well. Three trades under one badge, which is how somebody who came in for a fade ends up leaving with a consultation booked.

Today

The team keeps growing

We train new barbers here ourselves, and they look after you with care and Dominican energy, as José puts it. There is a drink for you while you wait.

MÁS DOS 8

Spanish for more than two, because in life we are never alone. The eight on its side is infinity, a cycle with no end and no limits. The name carried the idea before the sign went up.
