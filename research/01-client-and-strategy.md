## 2. What is wrong with the current site, which is the pitch

1. **It is a Wix template doing nothing.** The hero is the word "MasDos8" set enormous in a
   default geometric sans, next to a snapshot of the empty shop.
2. **Prices are hidden.** Every price above is real and published, but only inside
   `/book-online`. The homepage shows three unpriced category tiles. A barber's customer wants
   to know the cost of a beard trim before they commit to anything.
3. **The booking page is a product list.** Wix Bookings renders it like an eshop: repeated
   "Zjistit více / 300 českých korun / 300 Kč / Rezervovat nyní" blocks, with the price stated
   twice in two formats on every row.
4. **Typos and mis-filing in the live price list.** "Pernamentní makeup" (should be
   Permanentní), "drelock" (dreadlock), "Střední (malé) tetování" reads as "Medium (small)
   tattoo", and "mali tetvani" at 1,000 Kč is filed under permanent makeup. The address says
   "Chequia", which is Spanish for Czechia, on a Czech-language page.
5. **The language is unresolved.** Czech navigation, a Spanish review carousel, a Spanish
   chat greeting ("¿Cómo podemos ayudarte?"), and a Spanish footer credit.
6. **The work is not the hero.** For a barber and tattooist, the portfolio IS the product, and
   it sits in a secondary "Galerie" tab.
7. **4.8 from 225 reviews is not on the page.** That is the strongest asset the business owns.

## 3. Sector strategy: why a barbershop is not a gym

We built ten gym homepages before this. Almost none of that thinking transfers, and it is
worth being explicit about why, so the design is driven by the sector rather than by habit.

| | Gym (Blast & Burn) | Barber and tattoo (MasDos8) |
|---|---|---|
| The decision | Join a club. Considered, monthly, a commitment | Book one slot. Fast, repeat, low risk |
| What earns trust | The room, the equipment, the atmosphere | **One person's hands and their portfolio** |
| Hero should show | The space | **The work, and the man who does it** |
| Price role | A membership tier to compare | **A menu to scan.** Nobody compares tiers, they look up one number |
| Frequency | Daily for months | Every 3 to 5 weeks, forever |
| Emotion sold | Transformation, effort, discipline | Care, precision, a good half hour |
| Register | Loud, aggressive, high contrast | **Calm, crafted, grown-up.** Shouting at a barber's customer is wrong |
| Conversion | Enquire, then a sales conversation | **Book a time, right now, in two taps** |

### What follows from that, and should be true of every direction

1. **The booking is the spine, not a section.** A persistent way to book, visible at every
   scroll position. The gym could get away with "call us". This cannot.
2. **Three products, three journeys, routed early.**
   - *Barber*: price known, decision instant, books itself. Optimise for speed.
   - *Tattoo*: 6,000 to 18,000 Kč, needs the portfolio and a consultation, never an instant
     booking. Optimise for evidence and a conversation.
   - *PMU*: 1,500 to 5,000 Kč, a considered beauty decision, a different audience again.
   A single undifferentiated "services" list is the current site's core failure.
3. **The portfolio carries the page.** Cuts, beards, tattoos, brows. Photography is the
   product, so it should be large, plentiful and filterable by the three products.
4. **Put the prices on the homepage.** All of them. It is a competitive advantage that they
   are published at all, and it removes the main reason someone leaves.
5. **Name José Luis and put his face on it.** "Jose is a true artist" is in their reviews.
   People book a person.
6. **Use the 4.8 and the 225 loudly, with real quotes.** We have names and text, which the gym
   never gave us.
7. **The Latino thread is a real differentiator, and it is theirs, not ours.** "Happy to
   connect with the Latino community, felt like home" is a customer's own words. A Spanish
   speaking barber in Prague 4 serving Czechs, expats and the Latino community is a specific
   identity. It should be visible, and it argues for a genuine CZ / EN / ES language switch
   rather than the current accidental mix.
8. **Quiet, not loud.** Deep navy, oxblood, warm neutrals, real photography, generous type.
   The gym's job was to shout. This one's job is to look like the most competent chair in
   Prague 4.

### What must be avoided

- Barbershop cliché: crossed razors, handlebar-moustache line art, chalkboard textures,
  "Est. 20XX" ribbons, sepia everything. Every bad barber site in Europe already has these.
- Treating tattoo as a footnote. It is up to 18,000 Kč a booking, the highest value thing
  they sell.
- Any invented price, any invented review, any stock photograph presented as their work.
