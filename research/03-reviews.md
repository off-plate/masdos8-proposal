- **José Luis Castillo** is the owner. He is Dominican; two reviews name the flag and the
  Latino atmosphere, one names him in full.
- He personally does **cutting, tattooing and permanent makeup**, including microblading and
  eyeliner. Two separate PMU customers confirm it.
- There is at least one other barber, **Denny** (spelled Deny and Danny across reviews), and a
  woman barber praised as "precise, fast, she shaves and cuts great".
- A **free drink** on arrival is mentioned repeatedly: rum, whisky, coffee, or **mamajuana**,
  a Dominican drink, named by three separate customers.
- Manicure, pedicure and massage are mentioned by customers as available.
- One customer states every tenth haircut is free. That is a customer's claim, not a published
  offer, so it must not appear on the site as fact.

## The negatives, for honesty in the pitch

Three reviews complain about the booking system, not the craft: a confirmed reservation with
the door closed and no answer on two phones (Jakub, 11 months), arriving on time and still
waiting while barbers sat idle (Galin), and a booked barber swapped without warning
(Mario Hrvačić). **This is the single strongest commercial argument for the reservation system
we are proposing.** The work is not the problem, the scheduling is.
