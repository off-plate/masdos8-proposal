# Tone of voice, taken from their own Czech site

Michael, 2026-08-12: the proposal copy "screams AI slop", and it does. This file is the
correction. **Read it before writing a single line for this client.**

## What they actually sound like

Verbatim from masdos8.com:

- "Krásné výsledky. Osobní přístup."
- "Služby na nejvyšší úrovni. Reálné výsledky."
- "Nechte se inspirovat našimi exkluzivními díly."
- "Kousek naší historie."
- "...vášeň pro tento obor má doslova v krvi."
- "...než si splnil sen a otevřel vlastní."
- "Náš tým stále roste. Pravidelně školíme nové barberisty, kteří se o vás postarají s péčí
  a dominikánskou energií."

Five things characterise it:

1. **Warm and proud, never ironic.** They are pleased with their work and they say so plainly.
2. **First person plural.** "Náš tým", "postarají se o vás". The shop speaks as a we, to a you.
3. **Short declarative pairs.** "Krásné výsledky. Osobní přístup." Two nouns, two full stops.
4. **Invitational.** "Nechte se inspirovat." They ask you in.
5. **Feeling is allowed.** Passion in the blood, a dream fulfilled, Dominican energy. A British
   copywriter would cut all of that. Do not cut it. It is the brand.

## What I wrote instead, and why it was wrong

| Mine | The problem |
|---|---|
| "You are booking a person, not a slot" | The banned X-not-Y construction, and a swipe at other shops |
| "Priced by size, not by the hour" | Same construction |
| "Nobody hands you on halfway through" | Cold, and defined by what others do badly |
| "No handing you between people, no asking what it costs at the till" | Two negatives in one line |
| "Photographed by them, not bought from a library" | Same construction, and inward-looking |
| "The trade is in his blood rather than on his CV" | Clever at the client's expense |
| "One chair. One hand. Every price on it." | Clipped to the point of coldness |
| "Every price, before you sit" | Implies a warning |

The pattern: I wrote a knowing British agency voice that scores points against competitors.
They speak warmly about their own work and never mention anyone else.

## The rules for this client

- **Never** the X-not-Y construction, in any form: "not a slot", "rather than", "instead of a".
  This is a standing global rule and it was broken on nearly every page.
- Never define the shop by what other shops get wrong.
- Say what you get, warmly, in plain words. "A cut, a shave, a drink while you wait."
- Use we and you.
- Feeling is on. Passion, pride, family, energy, welcome.
- Keep sentences short, but let them be kind rather than clipped.
- No em dashes. No superlatives we cannot source. Every number stays real.
- English carries the Czech warmth across; it does not become a translation of it.

## Their own lines, in English, safe to use

- Beautiful results. A personal approach.
- Let us show you what we have made.
- A piece of our history.
- The passion is in his blood.
- He worked in other salons until he could open his own.
- Our team keeps growing. We train new barbers ourselves, and they will look after you with
  care and Dominican energy.
